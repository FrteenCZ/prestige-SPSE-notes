# 2. Sestavte kvadratickou rovnici o kořenech $x_{1,2}=2\pm 3i$. 
$$
\begin{gathered}
\begin{matrix}
-b=x_{1}+x_{2} && c=x_{1}x_{2} \\
-b=2+3i+2-3i && c=(2+3i)(2-3i) \\
-b=4 && c=2^{2}+3^{2}i^{2} \\
b=-4 && c=4+9=13
\end{matrix} \\
\boxed{x^{2}-4x+13=0}
\end{gathered}
$$

# 6. Určete druhý kořen rovnice $8x^{2}-2x-3=0$, znáte-li její kořen $x_{1}=\dfrac{3}{4}$.
$$
\begin{align}
x^{2}-\frac{1}{4}x-\frac{3}{8}=0 \\
 \\
-\left( -\frac{1}{4} \right)=x_{1}+x_{2} \\
-\frac{3}{8}=x_{1}x_{2} \\
 \\
\frac{1}{4}=\frac{3}{4}+x_{2} \\
x_{2}=\frac{1}{4}-\frac{3}{4} \\
\boxed{x_{2}=-\frac{1}{2}}
\end{align}
$$

# 2.Vypočtěte $\tan(x+y)$, je-li $\tan x=\frac{5}{6},\tan y=-\frac{1}{11}$.
$$
\begin{align}
x=\arctan \frac{5}{6} \\
y=\arctan \frac{-1}{11} \\
\tan\left( \arctan \left( \frac{5}{6} \right)+\arctan \left( -\frac{1}{11} \right) \right) = \boxed{\frac{49}{71} \approx 0.69}
\end{align}
$$

## b) $y=2+\cos\left( \frac{x}{2}+\pi \right)$
$$
\begin{align}
y=2+\cos\left( \frac{x}{2}+\pi \right) \\
y=2+\cos\left( \frac{1}{2}(x+2\pi) \right)
\end{align}
$$

- perioda = $\frac{2\pi}{\frac{1}{2}}=4\pi$

v pravoúhlém $\triangle$ $ABC$ známe:
- $t_{a}=\pu{ 17cm }$
- $t_{b}=\pu{ 15cm }$
- $a,b,c=?$
$$
\begin{align}
t_{a}=\sqrt{ \frac{a^{2}}{4}+b^{2} } \\
t_{b}=\sqrt{ a^{2}+\frac{b^{2}}{4} } \\
 \\
17^2=\frac{a^{2}}{4}+b^{2} \\
15^2=a^{2}+\frac{b^{2}}{4} \\
 \\
17^{2}=\frac{15^{2}-\frac{b^{2}}{4}}{4}+b^{2} \\
17^2\cdot16=15^{2}\cdot4-b^{2}+16b^{2} \\
17^{2}\cdot16-15^{2} \cdot4=15b^{2} \\
b=\pm\sqrt{ \frac{17^{2}\cdot16 - 15^{2} \cdot 4}{15} } \\
b=14\sqrt{ 285 }\approx \pu{ 15.8cm } \\
a\approx \pu{ 12.8cm } \\
c\approx\pu{ 20.3cm }
\end{align}
$$

- $a=\pu{ 13cm }$
- $b=\pu{ 14cm }$

$$
\begin{align}
a^{2}=b^{2}+c^{2}-2bc\cdot \cos \alpha \\
\alpha=\arccos \frac{a^{2}-b^{2}-c^{2}}{-2bc} \\
\alpha=\arccos \frac{13^{2}-14^{2}-15^{2}}{-2\cdot14\cdot 15} \\
\alpha \approx 53.1°
\end{align}
$$

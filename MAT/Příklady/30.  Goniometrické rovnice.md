# 2. Řešte rovnici: $1-\cos 2x+\sin x=0$
$$
\begin{align}
1-\cos 2x+\sin x=0 \\
1-\cos^{2}x+\sin^{2}x + \sin x=0 \\
2\sin ^{2}x+\sin x=0 \\
\sin x(2\sin x+1)=0 \\
\sin x=0 && 2\sin x=-1 \\
x=k\pi && \sin x=-\frac{1}{2} \\
&& x=\frac{11}{6}\pi+2k\pi \\
&& x= \frac{7}{6}+2k\pi \\
\boxed{x \in \left\{  k\pi, \frac{11}{6}\pi+2k\pi, \frac{7}{6}+2k\pi  \right\}}
\end{align}
$$

# 5. Řešte rovnici: $\sin ^2  x-6 \cdot \cos ^2  x+ \frac{1}{2}   \cdot \sin 2x=0$
$$
\begin{align}
\sin ^2  x-6 \cdot \cos ^2  x+ \frac{1}{2}   \cdot \sin 2x=0 \\
\sin ^{2}x-6 \cdot \cos ^{2}x+\sin x \cdot \cos x = 0 \\
\frac{\sin ^{2}x}{\cos ^{2}x}-6+ \frac{\sin x \cdot \cos x}{\cos ^{2}x}=0 && \cos x \neq 0\\
\tan ^{2}x-6+\tan x=0 \\
(\tan x+3)(\tan x-2)=0 \\
\tan x=-3 && \tan x=2 \\
x=\arctan(-3)+k\pi && x=\arctan(2)+k\pi \\
\boxed{x=\{ \arctan(-3)+k\pi , \arctan(2)+k\pi \}}
\end{align}
$$

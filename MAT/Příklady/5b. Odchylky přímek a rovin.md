# 1. Vypočtěte odchylku přímek:
## a) $p:x - 3y+6=0$ a $q:x+2y - 8=0$

$$
\begin{align}
\vec{p}=(1,-3) \\
\vec{q}=(1,2) \\
\alpha=\arccos\left( \frac{|\vec{p}\cdot \vec{q}|}{||\vec{p}||\cdot||\vec{q}||} \right) \\
\alpha=\arccos\left( \frac{|1-6|}{\sqrt{ 10 }\cdot \sqrt{ 5 }} \right) \\
\alpha=\arccos\left( \frac{5}{5\sqrt{ 2 }} \right) \\
\boxed{\alpha=\frac{\pi}{4}}
\end{align}
$$
## b) $p:x=1+t;~y= - 4t;~z= - 3+t$ a  $q:x=2s;~y= - 2 - 2s;~z= - s$
$$
\begin{align}
\vec{p}=(1,-4,1) \\
\vec{q}=(2,-2,-1) \\
\beta=\arccos\left( \frac{|\vec{p}\cdot \vec{q}|}{||\vec{p}||\cdot||\vec{q}||} \right) \\
\beta=\arccos\left( \frac{|2+8-1|}{3\sqrt{ 2 }\cdot 3} \right) \\
\beta=\arccos\left( \frac{9}{9\sqrt{ 2 }} \right) \\
\boxed{\beta=\frac{\pi}{4}}
\end{align}
$$

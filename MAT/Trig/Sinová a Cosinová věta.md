# Sinová
$$
\begin{gathered}
\frac{a}{\sin \alpha}=\frac{b}{\sin \beta}=\frac{c}{\sin \gamma}\\
\Downarrow \\
\frac{a}{b}=\frac{\sin\alpha}{\sin \beta} \\
\frac{b}{c}=\frac{\sin \beta}{\sin \gamma} \\
\frac{c}{a}=\frac{\sin \gamma}{\sin \alpha}
\end{gathered}
$$
